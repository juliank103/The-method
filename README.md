# The Method

Goal: $2,000/month, legal, reliable, with no ongoing manual work.

Starting point: under $5k of capital, one or two days available for setup, no
existing skills to sell, living in Illinois.

## The honest answer

With those inputs, **nothing legal can reliably pay $2,000/month on autopilot
starting now.** The three requirements cancel each other out:

| You can have…                 | …but it costs                                  |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Legal + reliable + hands-off  | Capital: about **$600,000** invested           |
| Legal + hands-off, no capital | Reliability: most attempts earn $0             |
| Legal + reliable, no capital  | Your time: a job or a business you run         |

Why $600k: a diversified portfolio can safely pay out about 4% a year
(the "4% rule"). Treasuries and money-market funds pay about the same, though
that rate moves. $2,000 × 12 ÷ 0.04 = $600,000.

Anything that claims to do this with $5k and a weekend is a scam or a lottery
ticket. See the [red flags](#red-flags) section below.

## The plan

The plan has two parts. Part 1 needs a few hours of one-time setup, and after
that it runs without you. It's the only reliable route to hands-off income.
Part 2 is optional and covers the income gap in the meantime.

### Part 1: The automatic engine (one-time setup, ~3 hours)

1. **Open a high-yield savings account** (Ally, Marcus, Capital One 360, or
   similar, FDIC-insured). Move about $1,000–$2,000 of your $5k into it as an
   emergency buffer. Without this buffer, one bad month forces you to sell
   your investments.
2. **Open a Roth IRA** at Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard. Investment growth and
   qualified withdrawals are tax-free, and Illinois doesn't tax retirement
   income anyway. If your employer doesn't offer a retirement plan, you may
   already be enrolled in **Illinois Secure Choice**, a state Roth IRA. Check
   your pay stub and either use it or opt out in favor of your own IRA.
3. **Put the rest of the $5k into one fund:** a target-date index fund, or
   a total-world index fund (e.g. VT, or FZROX + FZILX at Fidelity). Turn on
   automatic dividend reinvestment.
4. **Set up an automatic transfer** from checking to the IRA every payday,
   for whatever amount you can sustain. Raise it whenever your income goes
   up. When you hit the IRA's annual limit, send the overflow to a regular
   taxable brokerage account in the same fund.
5. **Don't touch it.** No trading, no checking it daily, no selling in
   crashes. The automation only works if you leave it alone.

How long until it pays $2,000/month in today's dollars (assuming a 5% return
after inflation and a $5k start):

| Monthly contribution | Years to $600k |
|---------------------:|---------------:|
| $250                 | ~46            |
| $500                 | ~35            |
| $1,000               | ~25            |
| $2,000               | ~16            |

You can run `python3 calculator.py` with your own numbers.

The contribution is what moves the timeline. Fund choice barely matters.

### Part 2: Low-effort income while it builds (optional)

These are legal and need little upkeep, but none of them is passive and none
will get you to $2,000 on its own:

- **Rent unused space.** Through Neighbor.com, a spare garage, driveway, or
  parking spot can bring in roughly $50–$300/month. Check your lease or HOA
  rules first.
- **Earn more at your job.** For most people, a raise, a certification, or
  switching employers is the fastest legal path to an extra $2,000/month.
  Every extra dollar you send to Part 1 shortens the timeline.
- **Learn a skill you can sell.** Without one, there's no business to build.
  If you want a business later, start by learning a skill (bookkeeping,
  basic web development, a trade). Then automate the business once it exists.
  Automating a business that doesn't exist yet doesn't work.

## Red flags

Skip anything that matches these patterns. Each one claims to deliver what
you asked for, and the legal ones almost never do.

- **"Done-for-you" Amazon, Shopify, dropshipping, or "AI e-commerce
  automation" stores.** The FTC has shut down several of these. The seller
  makes money from your fee, and you don't.
- **Trading bots, forex or crypto signals, "AI trading," arbitrage bots, or
  fixed-yield crypto staking.** If a bot reliably made money, its owner
  wouldn't sell it to you.
- **"YouTube automation" or "faceless channel" coaching.** You pay for the
  course, and the channel usually earns little or nothing.
- **Getting paid to receive and reship packages, or to move money through
  your bank account.** This is money muling. It's illegal, even if you didn't
  know.
- **Car-wrap ad offers that send you a check first.** This is a fake-check
  scam.
- **Any guaranteed monthly return**, MLM, or "passive income" course that
  costs more than a few dollars.
